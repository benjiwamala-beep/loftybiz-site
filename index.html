About

LoftyBiz is a YouTube channel by Wamala Benjamin, from Uganda. It is about building a business that can keep going without depending on you for every decision.

The videos cover everyday work such as finding customers, shaping an offer, setting prices, selling, marketing, and managing money — practical steps you can put in place, without the hype.

LoftyBiz is general business education. It is not financial, legal, or tax advice.
