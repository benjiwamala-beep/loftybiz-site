Everything Systex does

From mapping your business to running checklists every day, here is each part of Systex, with real screens from the demo bakery.

Features

🏠 One dashboard for every system

See what Sy noticed, how many systems you have, their average health, the owner hours you can win back, what to build next, reviews due, adoption and owner scorecards, all on one screen.

🗺️ Business Map

Chat with Systex about your business and it builds the map: departments, roles and people, tools, the client journey and every process. It flags single points of failure and owner bottlenecks.

🎯 Prioritizer and 90-day plan

Every process is scored on revenue impact, frequency, cost of errors, owner dependency and documentation. You get a ranked backlog, a 90-day sprint plan and the hours you can take back.

📘 SOP Builder

Structured SOPs with purpose, trigger, steps and roles. Each has an owner, review date, status and health score, with an AI quality check, audio summary, versions, training and publishing to Notion and Google Docs.

✅ Checklists you run every day

Turn any SOP into a checklist with an assignee and due date. Tick steps, log deviations and complete the run. Recurring runs handle daily, weekly and monthly work.

🎓 Training, quizzes and sign-off

Assign training from your SOPs. Quizzes are generated for you, and sign-off only happens after passing. See adoption for every person.

💬 Ask Systex

Your team asks “how do we…?” and gets the answer from your own SOPs, with citations that jump to the exact step. Questions with no answer become gaps to fill.

📊 Meeting rhythm and scorecard

Weekly numbers, quarterly priorities, issues, to-dos and accountability seats, plus a weekly leadership meeting agenda filled in for you.

🧭 Mind maps

See every system by area, owner or status. Tap a branch to fold it, tap a system to open it.

📦 Industry template packs

Start fast with ready systems for agencies, e-commerce, professional services, trades and clinics, adapted to your business through a short interview.

🏅 Systems Champion course

Six modules with lessons and quizzes for the person who owns systems in your business, with an 80% pass mark and a printable certificate.

The complete feature list

Everything planned for Systex, grouped by what it helps you do. Items marked “Planned” are on the roadmap after launch.

🗺️ Business Map: see your whole business

  • Chat onboarding that captures your departments, roles, people, tools, client journey and processes
  • Client journey map from first lead to delivery to renewal
  • Links every process to its owner, tools, inputs, outputs and risks
  • Single-point-of-failure finder (“only one person knows payroll”)
  • Owner-bottleneck finder and dependency list
  • Mind maps of your systems by area, owner or status, plus a map for each knowledge topic
  • Full business knowledge graph and “what if” simulations Planned

🎯 Prioritizer: know what to systemize first

  • Scores each process on revenue impact, frequency, cost of errors, owner dependency and how well it is documented
  • Ranked backlog of the minimum viable systems your business needs
  • Ready-made 90-day sprint plan
  • “Get the owner out” view that shows the hours you can win back
  • Benchmarks that compare your progress with similar businesses Planned

🎙️ Extraction Studio: get know-how out of people's heads

  • AI interviews by chat or voice, with smart follow-ups (why, what if, what are the exceptions)
  • Interview questions can be read aloud
  • Interview links your team can answer without logging in, shared by Slack, email or SMS
  • Mobile voice capture for field staff
  • Import meeting transcripts, notes, documents, files, chat threads and screen-recording exports
  • Turns raw input into an SOP draft with steps, a checklist, decision points, a decision tree and a swimlane
  • Live meeting listening and video capture Planned

📘 SOP Builder & Library

  • Structured SOPs: purpose, trigger, steps, roles, tools, quality checks, decision points and exceptions
  • An owner, review date and status on every SOP
  • Approval workflow
  • Full version history with one-click restore
  • AI quality check for missing steps, vague wording and unclear ownership
  • Export to Markdown, Word (DOCX) or JSON
  • Publish to Notion and Google Docs
  • Two-way sync, Confluence and SharePoint publishing, and translation Planned

✅ Adoption, training & accountability

  • One owner per system, with review reminders and gentle nudges
  • Training assignments created from your SOPs
  • Auto-generated quizzes, with sign-off only after passing
  • Adoption tracking for every person
  • Health score for every system: freshness, training, use and open issues, shown as color badges
  • Owner scorecards
  • Weekly Champion digest and a monthly report for the owner

▶️ Execution: run your systems every day

  • Run any SOP as a checklist with an assignee and due date
  • Recurring runs for daily, weekly and monthly work
  • Log deviations, which feed improvement suggestions
  • Start runs from Zapier, Make or webhooks
  • Deviation analytics Planned

📅 Leadership meeting rhythm

  • Weekly scorecard with the numbers that matter
  • Quarterly priorities
  • Issues list and to-dos
  • Accountability seats: who owns what
  • Weekly leadership meeting agenda, pre-filled from all of the above

🧩 Industry template packs

  • Ready systems for agencies, e-commerce, professional services, trades and home services, and clinics and health practices
  • Five starter systems in each pack, in original Systex wording
  • AI adapts a template to your business through a short interview
  • Template marketplace Planned

🧠 Built-in expert business knowledge

  • A built-in library of proven business know-how: frameworks, steps, principles, metrics, examples and common mistakes
  • Compiles a ready-made system from that knowledge, adapted to your Business Map
  • Add your own knowledge sources: websites, Google Docs and Slides, PDFs, audio files and pasted text
  • Audio summaries you can listen to or download as MP3
  • Offline knowledge search that works without internet

💬 Ask Systex: answers from your own systems

  • Ask “how do we…?” and get an answer from your SOPs
  • Clickable citations that jump to the exact SOP step
  • Questions it can't answer are logged as gaps to fill
  • Ask Systex inside Slack with a slash command
  • Ask by typing or by voice
  • Microsoft Teams bot Planned

🏆 Systems Champion workspace & certification

  • A home base for the person who owns systemization: backlog, interviews in progress, reviews due, lowest-health systems, adoption, gaps and reports
  • Systems Champion course: six modules with lessons and quizzes
  • 80% pass mark and progress tracking
  • Verifiable, printable certificate

🤝 Coach & partner hub

  • Multi-client dashboard for coaches and consultants
  • Create client workspaces with template packs in a few clicks
  • Coach directory where businesses can book a certified coach
  • Done-with-you 90-day sprint playbook in a shared workspace
  • White-label option and a coach marketplace Planned

✨ An alive, interactive experience

  • Bright color emojis throughout, with health badges you can read at a glance
  • Smooth animations, friendly pop-up messages and confetti when you hit a win
  • Drag-and-drop
  • Dark and light themes

🔐 Platform & security

  • Accounts and multiple workspaces
  • Roles: owner, admin, champion, member and coach

Dark mode, too

Switch between light and dark themes whenever you like, on the web and on your desktop.
