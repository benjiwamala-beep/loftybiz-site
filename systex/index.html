The problem: the business runs on you

  • Every question comes to you. When you step away, things slow down or go wrong.
  • How the work gets done lives in people's heads. When someone leaves, the know-how leaves with them.
  • Documents get written once, then go out of date, and nobody owns them.
  • You know you need systems, but not which ones to build first or how to get the team to use them.

The solution: Systex

Systex maps how your business really works, shows which systems matter most, and interviews your people to pull the know-how out of their heads.

It turns that into clear SOPs and checklists, gives each one an owner and a review date, trains the team with quizzes and sign-off, and scores the health of every system so nothing quietly falls apart.

Sy guides you the whole way, remembers your goals and celebrates your progress.
