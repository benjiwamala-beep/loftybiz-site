Questions

Answers to common questions about Systex: launch, phones, voice, data, tools you already use and how to join early access.

What is Systex?

Systex is the business-systems platform from LoftyBiz. It helps you map your business, decide which systems to build first, capture know-how from your team, turn it into SOPs and checklists, and make sure owners keep them alive.

When can I use it?

Systex is coming soon. Join the early-access list and we will let you know first.

How much will it cost?

We will share plans and pricing with the early-access list first.

Do I need technical skills?

No. You chat with Sy, by typing or by voice, and Systex does the heavy lifting.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. There are apps for Android and iPhone, and you can install Systex on your desktop too.

Can I talk to it?

Yes. Tap the mic to talk to Sy or Ask Systex. Sy replies in text, and speaks out loud only if you turn that on.

Will it replace the tools I already use?

No. Systex works alongside them: it publishes to Notion and Google Docs, answers questions in Slack and connects to Zapier and Make.

Is my data safe?

Systex has roles, an audit log, single sign-on and encrypted key storage, and it is designed so your business data is never used to train AI models.

How does it connect to the LoftyBiz videos?

Systex is where the systems from the LoftyBiz lessons come to life. Until it launches, use the free worksheets that go with each long video.

I'm a coach or consultant. Is it for me?

Yes. The coach hub lets you run many clients from one dashboard. Write to us to talk about it.
